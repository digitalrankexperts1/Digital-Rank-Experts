How We Started

Founded on a simple frustration: SEO reporting that says nothing.

Digital Rank Experts was founded by Sohail Ahmad in Lahore after years of watching businesses pay for SEO retainers that produced glossy reports and little ranking movement. We built the agency we wished existed: senior strategists, transparent live tracking, and a bias toward shipping fixes over flagging them.

Today we work with local businesses, national e-commerce brands, and service companies across Pakistan and abroad — each on a plan sized to their market, not a one-size template.

Our Approach

  • Audit first, quote second — we don't sell what a site doesn't need.
  • Every fix is tracked against a live keyword tracker your team can see.
  • Development and SEO sit on the same team, so fixes ship the same week.
  • Reporting explains "why," not just "what."
